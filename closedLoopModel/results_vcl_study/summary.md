# Study on the October closed-loop tests (VClosedLoop)

Configuration `config_vcl_study.yaml`; base model `config_vcl.yaml`. Generated by `vcl_study.py`. Only the files of `LucyIdentification/VClosedLoop` are used.

## 1-2. Controller transfer functions

Built from `Parameters_*.akd` with the structure of the AKD2G loops (series PI velocity loop, biquads AR1-AR2 forward and AR3-AR4 on the feedback, observer as a first-order low pass of bandwidth OBS.BW, proportional position loop with PL.KI = 0, current PI with its zero on R/L). Control law I_cmd = C_v H_fwd [C_p (P_cmd - P_fb) - H_fb s P_fb]; C_e = C_v H_fwd C_p from the position error, C_fb = C_v H_fwd (C_p + s H_fb) from the encoder position, C_WB = C_v H_fwd (1 + 2 pi PL.KP / s) the WorkBench Controller trace. Polynomials in s, descending powers; A per rad, A per rad/s.

## 3-5. Plant parameters on the October frequency responses

Measured plant: encoder velocity over current command of `BodeMeasurement_*.csv`; model: H6 with the motor, the current loop and the ground constraint k(1 + j eta). Residuals ln(G_model/G_measured) (magnitude and phase) on the points of the band, robust loss; parameters on their logarithms. Ring: stiffness factor kappa_r of gimbal_mdof (1 = ring alone, H6 = 1000), acting on k_theta,1 (encoder - hub) and k_theta,2 (hub - motor) as a compliance in series. Dampers: element dampers of the lumped model, with a weak prior on their start values (H6, zeta_1 = 0.10) for those the band does not determine.
