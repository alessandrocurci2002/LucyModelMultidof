## Notes

- Model: closed loop of `main.py` with the base configuration; the estimate replaces Kpp, Kpv, Kiv of the drives and the Coulomb level of the Dahl friction (sigma0 of H6 kept); filters, observer, current loop and limits of the .akd files.
- Windows start at rest from the measurement: encoder positions, holding currents carried by the friction state, integrators consistent with them; the telescope is balanced (g = 0).
- Fit NRMSE as in the System Identification Toolbox (100 = perfect, 0 = the mean of the measurement).
