## Assumptions

- Drive: series PI loops with the conventions checked on the WorkBench Controller trace; current PI with its integral zero on the electrical pole R/L (crossover IL.KP/L); continuous-time model, no sampling or computation delay; observer as a first-order low pass of bandwidth OBS.BW (Velocity Loop page).
- Motor: field-oriented, ideal commutation, per-phase rms model; no cogging; static friction of the datasheet not modelled (bearing friction is the Dahl element).
- Plant: nonlinear Newton-Euler model of gimbal_mdof; telescope balanced (g = 0); bearing friction in the time domain as Dahl elements equivalent to the H6 ground constraint at the test amplitude.
- Measured traces: WorkBench exports; the model of section 3 uses the tuning recorded in them.
